# Glossary

Quick reference for acronyms, European Union grant conventions, evaluation units, and organisations referenced across this repository.

---

## Project & EU grant terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **aWISH** | *Animal Welfare Indicators at the Slaughterhouse* — an €8.0m Horizon Europe project (grant `101060818`, Nov 2022 – Oct 2026) coordinated by ILVO. Tested automated welfare cameras and sensors in six European pig and broiler slaughterhouses to feed scores back to farms. |
| **AWI** | *Animal Welfare Indicator*. The **AWI Catalogue** (deliverable D2.5) lists indicators that can be measured automatically at slaughter. |
| **CORDIS** | *Community Research and Development Information Service*, the European Commission's public database of EU-funded research projects ([grant 101060818](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101060818)). |
| **Deliverable (`Dx.y`)** | A formal project report submitted to the European Commission, numbered by work package (`x`) and report (`y`). Frequently cited here: **D1.1 / D1.2** (communication & exploitation plans), **D1.4** (project website report), **D1.6** (pilot practice abstracts, including TRLs), **D1.8** (policy briefs & Sep 2025 workshop slides), **D2.1** (valid indicators literature review), **D2.2** (sensor methodology review), **D2.5** (AWI Catalogue), **D4.2** (welfare assessment & improvement results), **D4.3** (Best Practice Guides), **D5.2** (value-chain actors' needs & constraints), and **D6.2 / D6.3** (Data Management Plans v1 and v2). |
| **DMP** | *Data Management Plan* (deliverables D6.2 and D6.3), recording data ownership, openness, and retention rules per pilot. |
| **DSA** | *Data Sharing Agreement* between participating farms/plants and project partners (32 signed across pilots per D6.3). |
| **TRL** | *Technology Readiness Level*, an EU 1–9 maturity scale ranging from **1** (basic principle observed) through **3–5** (early/lab/pilot prototype) to **9** (proven in commercial operation). |
| **WP** | *Work Package* in a Horizon Europe project: **WP1** communication & dissemination, **WP2** indicators & sensors, **WP3** the six slaughterhouse pilots, **WP4** data platform, feedback tool & welfare assessment, **WP5** value-chain actors, **WP6** project & data management. |

## Evaluation & cost-effectiveness terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AIM** | *Ambitious Impact*, an animal-welfare charity incubator and research organisation. |
| **AIM's bar** | The cost-effectiveness threshold AIM uses when evaluating new animal-welfare interventions: **30 SADs averted per US dollar** (comparable across species, per AIM's lead animal welfare researcher, 18 Nov 2026). |
| **CEA** | *Cost-Effectiveness Analysis* — here, expected suffering-adjusted days (SADs) averted per US dollar spent on a $1M programme. |
| **Counterfactual** | What would happen *without* a successor programme. Programmes are credited only with the difference they make (higher probability of a rule passing, or bringing it forward in time). |
| **SADs** | *Suffering-Adjusted Days*, the impact unit used by AIM: disabling-equivalent days of animal suffering averted, weighted by pain intensity, species welfare range, and probability of sentience. |
| **WFM** | *Weighted-Factor Model* — the 7-factor (0–10) scoring framework used in Section 3 of the Founder Brief to rank the 19 technologies aWISH tested. |

## Animal welfare, slaughterhouse & statistical terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Contact dermatitis** | Painful skin burns on broiler chickens caused by wet, ammonia-rich litter on farm; manifests mainly as **footpad dermatitis (FPD)** and **hock burn**, both visible on the carcass line at slaughter. |
| **FPD** | *Footpad Dermatitis* — lesions on the bottom of broiler feet, scored automatically after plucking by systems such as ChickenCheck. |
| **ICC** | *Intraclass Correlation Coefficient* — measures agreement between different human scorers (inter-rater) or repeat scores by the same scorer (intra-rater). |
| **κ (Cohen's kappa)** | Statistical measure of agreement between two raters (or a camera and human raters) adjusted for chance agreement (`1.0` = perfect agreement). |
| **Lairage** | Holding pens at the slaughterhouse where animals rest between unloading from trucks and moving to the stunner. |
| **Malus** | A financial penalty (price deduction per kg or per animal) applied to a supplier when slaughter-line welfare scores exceed a threshold. |
| **PHMS** | *Pig Health Monitoring System* — manual lung-lesion scoring protocol used as the human reference standard for Lesion-Detect. |
| **Stun-to-stick interval** | Time elapsed between an animal leaving the stunning chamber (e.g., CO₂ gondola) and bleeding (sticking). Longer intervals raise the risk of animals regaining consciousness. |

## Organisations, institutes & welfare schemes

| Name | Role |
|---|---|
| **Beter Leven** | Major Dutch animal welfare certification label, run by **Dierenbescherming** (Dutch Society for the Protection of Animals). |
| **BioSense Institute** | Research institute in Serbia; scientific co-lead of Pilot 6 (Carnex) and host/maintainer of the aWISH website. |
| **CIWF** | *Compassion in World Farming*, an international farm-animal welfare NGO. |
| **CLK GmbH** | German computer-vision vendor behind **ChickenCheck** (broiler footpad, hock burn, catch damage, scratch) and **PigInspector** (pig carcass lesions and tail length). |
| **DG SANTE** | European Commission Directorate-General for Health and Food Safety (leads EU animal welfare legislation). |
| **DMRI / DTI** | Danish Meat Research Institute / Danish Technological Institute (*Teknologisk Institut*); owns **Nuna Solutions** (developer of the BlinkAI pig stunning sensor). |
| **EAAP** | *European Federation of Animal Science* (hosted the aWISH Final Event in Hamburg, 10 Sep 2026). |
| **Eurogroup for Animals** | Brussels-based umbrella advocacy group representing EU animal welfare NGOs. |
| **FBN** | *Research Institute for Farm Animal Biology* (Germany); developed the **STREMODO** stress-vocalisation monitor and IGF-BP blood assay. |
| **IGBZ PAN** | *Institute of Genetics and Animal Biotechnology of the Polish Academy of Sciences*; scientific co-lead of Pilot 4 (Plukon Sieradz). |
| **ILVO** | *Flanders Research Institute for Agriculture, Fisheries and Food* (Belgium); coordinator of aWISH and holder of the tear-staining camera. |
| **Itavi** | French poultry technical institute; scientific co-lead of Pilot 3 (DUC) and developer of the **EBENE** app and **EBroilerTrack** tools. |
| **NVWA** | *Netherlands Food and Consumer Product Safety Authority* (its scientific advisory office is **BuRO**). |
| **QS** | *Qualität und Sicherheit GmbH* — Germany's dominant meat quality-assurance scheme; mandates broiler footpad scoring and permits video analysis of pig tail findings. |
| **RSPCA / RSPCA Assured** | Royal Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (UK) and its farm-animal welfare assurance label. |
| **UAB** | *Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona*; scientific co-lead of Pilot 2 (Batallé) and co-developer of the 2D pig tail camera. |
| **UBITECH** | Technical partner responsible for the central aWISH data platform (WP4). |
| **VetMedUni** | *University of Veterinary Medicine, Vienna*; scientific co-lead of Pilot 5 (Grossfurtner-Higelsberger). |
