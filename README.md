# aWISH handover

What survives aWISH, an €8m Horizon Europe project (grant 101060818, November 2022 to October 2026) that put welfare cameras and sensors into six European slaughterhouses, and what a successor could do with it.

Written by Otto Tutzauer Hellström in the Sentient Futures Project Incubator 2026, mentored by Aaron Boddy (Ambitious Impact).

## How it was checked

Every claim on the founder brief and in packs A and B was checked by a person against the passage it rests on, as the Sentient Futures AI-use policy requires; the logs in `verification/` are the record. Verdicts: OK (supported), PAGE (supported, page number corrected), SECOND (rests on a secondary record only), EDIT (supported after rewording; the reworded text is in the file), WRONG (cut or corrected in the file), OPEN (source could not be reached at the time). Every EDIT and WRONG has been applied to the files here.

The logs cite the aWISH deliverables and papers by file name (`deliverables/…`, `papers/…`). Those files are not copied here: the deliverables are on [CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101060818) and the [project website](https://www.awish-project.eu/), and the papers are cited by DOI. References to 'private project notes (available on request)' point to working notes that are not published.

Left out on purpose: email addresses, notes about individuals, meeting notes, programme material, AIM's own SADs sheets, and third-party papers.

## AI use

Claude (Anthropic) was used for literature and web search, for extracting facts from the aWISH deliverables into the packs, for drafting the brief, the ranking scores and the cost-effectiveness model and its code, and for building the verification tool. The scope, the questions, the ranking factors and weights, the model's conventions and the outreach are the author's, as is every verdict in the logs. AI is not an author of this work. Details are in section 7 of the founder brief.
