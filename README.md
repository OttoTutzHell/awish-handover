# aWISH handover

What survives **[aWISH](https://www.awish-project.eu/)** (*Animal Welfare Indicators at the Slaughterhouse*), an €8.0m Horizon Europe project (grant `101060818`, Nov 2022 – Oct 2026) that installed automated welfare cameras and sensors in six European pig and broiler slaughterhouses to feed welfare scores back to farms—and what a successor project could do with what it left behind.

Written by Otto Tutzauer Hellström in the Sentient Futures Project Incubator 2026, mentored by Aaron Boddy ([Ambitious Impact](https://www.ambitiousimpact.com/)). Contact: **otto@tutzauer.se**.

---

## Bottom line

- **Only the broiler policy play clears the funding bar.** Using validated automated cameras for broiler footpad dermatitis and hock burn (CLK's **ChickenCheck**) to push for farm-level consequences in EU rules, certifier schemes, or retailer contracts averages an estimated **73 suffering-adjusted days (SADs) averted per $1** at a $1M tier—clearing Ambitious Impact's 30 SADs/$ bar. The bottleneck is policy enforcement, not hardware.
- **Pig technologies are a watch, not yet a play.** Estimated cost-effectiveness for pig carcass cameras (**PigInspector**: 4.5 SADs/$; **UAB tail camera**: 0.046 SADs/$) falls well short of the bar, and most pig slaughter-line sensors still lack peer-reviewed aWISH validation. Revisit once the consortium's final public reports (deliverable D4.2 and the work package 3 pilot results) are released.
- **Default outcome is no successor.** Without a founder by mid-2027, commercial vendors and national schemes will keep carrying broiler footpad cameras, while aWISH's pilot relationships, prototype hardware, and closed pilot data fade out.

## Where to find the original aWISH deliverables

The public aWISH deliverables (numbered `D1.1` through `D6.3`, plus the AWI Catalogue and event slide decks) and third-party journal papers are **not mirrored in this repository for copyright reasons**. All cited sources can be accessed directly via:

- **aWISH project website:** [www.awish-project.eu](https://www.awish-project.eu/) — public deliverables, AWI Catalogue, pilot profiles, journal papers (`/journal-papers/`), conference papers (`/conference-papers/`), and event slide decks.
- **EU CORDIS record:** [cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101060818](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101060818) — official grant listing, participants, deliverables, and publication record.
- **Zenodo & journal DOIs:** Conference abstracts, slide decks, and open-access papers are cited by DOI in [Pack A (Table 2)](handover-pack/A-what-validated.md#table-2-publications) and can be searched on [Zenodo under grant `101060818`](https://zenodo.org/search?q=101060818).

## Verification status & AI use

- **Claim-by-claim human check:** Every claim in the initial Founder Brief (135 claims) and initial Packs A and B (479 claims) was checked by a person against its primary source as of 17 Nov 2026, per the Sentient Futures AI-use policy; every `EDIT` and `WRONG` finding has been applied. Verdict codes: `OK` (supported), `PAGE` (supported, page number corrected), `EDIT` (supported after rewording), `WRONG` (cut or corrected), `SECOND` (secondary source only), `OPEN` (source unreachable).
- **Pending checks on recent updates:** Updates made on **19 Nov** (new Ambitious Impact parameters and ILVO answers, listed at the end of [`verification-log-founder-brief.md`](verification/verification-log-founder-brief.md)) and **22 Nov** (revised Packs A and B, marked **`[unverified]`** inline) have not yet been logged as verified. See [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md) for details.
- **AI use:** Claude (Anthropic) was used for literature/web search, extracting facts from aWISH deliverables, drafting the brief, ranking scores, cost-effectiveness model/code, and building the verification viewer. Scope, questions, ranking factors and weights, model conventions, outreach, and every verification verdict are the author's. AI is not an author of this work. Details are in Section 7 of the Founder Brief.
