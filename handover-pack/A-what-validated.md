# A. What aWISH has validated

This pack lists every welfare indicator and sensor the project covered and says, for each one, what validation exists: by whom, against what, and with what result.

## In brief

- **38 indicator–sensor rows**: 22 for pigs (P1–P22) and 16 for broilers (B1–B16).
- **Four rows have peer-reviewed validation figures from the aWISH period (2022–2026):**
	- P3 Weight-Detect (pig body weight, on farm)
	- P9 Lesion-Detect (pig lung lesions, slaughter line)
	- B10 and B11 ChickenCheck (broiler footpad dermatitis and hock burn, slaughter line)
- **Every other figure concerning pigs** is one of two kinds:
	- a pre-aWISH third-party study of a vendor system
	- for P8 and P10, unreviewed slides from aWISH's Final Event
- **The pilots' own final results (work package 3) are not public yet.** The pig validation statistics are expected before 2027 (see [Could not find](#could-not-find--not-stated)).

## How to read this pack

### The sources
aWISH's reports are called deliverables and numbered D*x.y*: *x* is the work package (WP), *y* the report. WP1 is communication and dissemination, WP2 indicators and sensors, WP3 the pilots. The ones this pack cites:

- **Page numbers** are PDF page numbers, and may be off by one or two. 
- **Quotes** are verbatim from the source.
- **"Not stated"** means the named sources were searched and say nothing on the point. The footnote says which pages were checked.
- **[unverified]** marks text that is not yet in the verification log.[^new]
- **The following D2.1 scores** are the deliverable's authors' estimates and "should therefore be treated with caution".[^d21-15]
	- Indicator validity: 0 to 2, "a three-point scale" for whether the indicator relates to welfare.
	  - Measurement validity: 
		  - 1 = "Correlation with the indicator (or 'manual' observation) (0.7 or higher)", 
		  - 0 = "Only interobserver reliability (below 0.7)".[^annex-labels] "?" means not scored.
	  - "Close to completion" is a TRL.

### The fields in each row

- **Stage** follows the source's own phase labels:
	- Catalogue: antemortem, stunning or postmortem at the slaughterhouse
	- D1.6: on farm, transport or slaughterhouse
- **Sensor** names the technology and its vendor. Every sensor is automated unless its entry says otherwise.
- **TRL** is the technology readiness level, from 1 (basic principle) to 9 (proven in commercial use). "est." marks a TRL that D2.2's authors estimated rather than the vendor stated.

### Caveats

1. D2.1 and D2.2 are literature and market reviews. All agreement figures in them and their annexes are *pre-aWISH third-party studies of vendor systems*, not aWISH pilot results. D2.2 states its validation criterion as (R>0.75), and/or "accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity."[^d22-10]
2. D2.2's TRLs carry a printed disclaimer: "The TRL is not directly stated by the provider/researcher, but that is estimated based on the information provided on the provider websites or in papers published".[^d22-19-25]
3. The Catalogue records only vendor-website descriptions. For broilers it adds a one-line "externally validated (ref)" note. It gives no aWISH pilot statistics.
4. D1.2 contains no TRLs. The per-pilot TRLs therefore come from D1.6,[^d16-9-11-range] the pilot technology list from D1.8,[^d18-list] and the publication list from the CORDIS listing.[^cordis]

#### P9. Lung (and liver) lesions

**Stage:** Postmortem (evisceration line)

**Sensors:**
- Lesion-Detect, camera-based (Innotech / InnoTech Vision): "Technology under development within the aWISH project".[^cat-60] Also the ADAL robot (Farm4Trade), not aWISH.[^p9-s]
- *Automation intended; in development.*

**Reported agreement with human scoring or a gold standard:**
- **Lesion-Detect: aWISH-period, peer-reviewed**[^ar26]
- two pilots against manual PHMS lung scoring: Batallé, Spain, hanging lungs (Trial 1, n = 102); Carnex, Serbia, lungs in trays (Trial 2, n = 228)
- false-positive and false-negative rates 2.50 % and 1.84 % of lung area in Trial 1, 3.28 % and 2.16 % in Trial 2

- **Caveats:**
	- agreement is reported as lesion-area overlap, not as the correlation or sensitivity/specificity D2.2 asks for
	- the first author and two co-authors are with the vendor (InnoTech Vision); the others are with the two pilot plants
- **Conference abstracts** (Zenodo 17630315, 2024; 17786812, BSE 2025) give no figures: 17,000 images collected and focus problems (2024); "manual scoring and the automated scoring were compared" (2025).
- **ADAL** (BSE-rev, pre-aWISH): "detect pleurisy with an accuracy of 85.5 % (specificity = 96 %, sensitivity = 92 %)" and "(r = 0.96)" vs veterinarians (Bonicelli et al. 2021).[^bse-10] ADAL TRL 8 (est.)[^d22-19]

#### B10. Foot pad dermatitis (carcass)

**Stage:** Postmortem (after plucking)

**Sensors:** ChickenCheck Footpad: camera (CLK GmbH), "5 classes of FPD … Approx. 5 pairs/second";[^cat-140] pilots 3 & 4. Also a Meyn prototype, not aWISH.[^b10-s]

**Reported agreement with human scoring or a gold standard:**
- **aWISH-period, peer-reviewed** (open access, CC BY)[^ps26]
- **Design:**
	- two plants: DUC (12,000 birds/h) and Plukon Sieradz (15,000 birds/h)
	- validation set of 500 images / 1,000 feet and hocks; three human assessors
	- footpad scored per QS Guideline (0, 1, 2a, 2b)
- **Results, footpad:**
	- rater-median vs camera kappa 0.70 (0.67–0.74); linear-weighted kappa 0.80

	- lesion-area R² 0.93; camera bias +2.47 %
- **Caveats (from the paper):**
	- score cut-offs are CLK-confidential
	- images carried no flock/batch identity, so no flock-level validation
	- CLK changed the software after the study
	- one author is a CLK employee
- **Meyn** (pre-aWISH): "(r=0.54 and 0.59) … reached r=0.69 and 0.74" (Vanderhasselt et al.)[^d22-33]
