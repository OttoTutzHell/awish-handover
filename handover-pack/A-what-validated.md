# A. What aWISH has validated — indicator × sensor source table

Rows carrying aWISH-period validation figures: **4** (P3 Weight-Detect, P9 Lesion-Detect, B10 ChickenCheck footpad, B11 ChickenCheck hock). Lesion-Detect has a peer-reviewed aWISH-period validation (Banhazi et al. 2026, Agronomy Research 24(2):76–95, published 25 May 2026). It is the only pig slaughterhouse sensor with one; every other pig-row figure is a pre-aWISH third-party study of a vendor system.

**Sources beyond the core deliverables.** Because D1.2 contains no TRLs, the per-pilot TRL statements were taken from `D1.6 Practice abstracts batch 1.txt` (pp.9–11), the pilot technology list from `D1.8 Policy briefs … (M36, Oct 2025).txt` (pp.25–30 of the text split), and the publication list from `CORDIS listing 101060818.txt` (pp.3–7). All three are cited by page; cells drawn from them are marked **[D1.6]**, **[D1.8]**, **[CORDIS]**. Web sources are cited by URL. Page = Nth form-feed-delimited chunk (page 1 before the first \f); for D2.1/D2.2/D1.6 this coincides with the printed page number.

**Caveats that apply to every row.** 
1. D2.1 and D2.2 are literature/market reviews; all agreement figures in them are *pre-aWISH third-party studies of vendor systems*, not aWISH pilot results (D2.2 p.10 states the validation criterion: "good correlation (R>0.75) … and/or … accuracy, sensitivity, and specificity"). 
2. D2.2 TRLs carry the printed disclaimer: "The TRL is not directly stated by the provider/researcher, but that is estimated based on the information provided on the provider websites or in papers published" (D2.2 p.19, p.25). 
3. The Catalogue records only vendor-website descriptions plus, for broilers, a one-line "externally validated (ref)" note; it gives no aWISH pilot statistics. 
4. The final results of aWISH's pilots (WP3) are not yet public. Three aWISH-period validations are peer-reviewed: ChickenCheck footpad and hock burn (Poultry Science 2026; B10, B11), Weight-Detect (Agronomy Research 2025; P3) and Lesion-Detect (Agronomy Research 2026; P9). "Stage" follows the source's own phase labels (Catalogue: antemortem / stunning / postmortem at the slaughterhouse; D1.6: on-farm / transport / slaughterhouse).
